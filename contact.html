Getting Here

How to arrive

By Private Car
Take Highway 304 east from Bangkok. Turn onto Route 3101 toward Pleangyao. The retreat is signposted 4km after the Pleangyao market. (90 min from Bangkok)
By Railway
Eastern Line from Hua Lamphong or Makkasan to Chachoengsao Junction (2 hrs). Our complimentary longtail boat service meets all arriving trains.
Complimentary Transfer
Guests staying 3 nights or more receive a complimentary luxury vehicle transfer from Suvarnabhumi or Don Mueang airports.
