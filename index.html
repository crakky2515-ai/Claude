Our Story

A sanctuary beyond time

Nestled within the lush landscape of Pleangyao district, Banrimklong Resort is more than a hotel — it is an act of preservation. Each pavilion has been carefully restored to honour the architectural language of old Chachoengsao, while offering every modern grace the discerning traveller requires.

Here, the mornings carry mist over the canals. The evenings dissolve slowly into amber light. And between those hours, everything — every detail — is attended to with quiet devotion.
